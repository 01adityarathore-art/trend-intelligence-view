# Trend Intelligence: read-only view

**Open it:** https://01adityarathore-art.github.io/trend-intelligence-view/

This is a snapshot from 28 Oct 2026 showing which designs are trending in India, for four categories: T-shirts, lehengas, tie-dye and pashmina. Switch between them inside the page.

- **Top 5 Designs.** Five designs per category where buyer intent is highest. Each one has a design sheet, an AI model photo, a price, a listing title and the evidence behind it.
- **Pattern Studio.** Prints and patterns ranked by a Trend Score from 0 to 100.
- **Keyword shelves.** The top buy-intent searches, each with the products Google Shopping shows for it.
- Search demand, audience by life stage, marketplaces, brands and social, all for each category.

The method is in [method.html](https://01adityarathore-art.github.io/trend-intelligence-view/method.html), or as a [PDF](method.pdf).

## Notes

- The model photos are AI images of people who don't exist. They were made with Z-Image Turbo (Apache-2.0).
- Product photos and captions belong to their sellers. Phone numbers and emails in captions are hidden.
- Search engines are told not to index this page.
